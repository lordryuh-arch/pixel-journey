# Pixel Journey

Create a beautiful, simple and highly nostalgic official landing page for a 2D pixel-art game called **IDLEMON REVO**.

CORE GOAL:
Design the website using principles of visual psychology and UX: low cognitive load, strong visual hierarchy, curiosity, nostalgia, clear CTAs, emotional storytelling and effortless navigation. The visitor should understand the game within 30 seconds and feel: “I want to explore this world.”

STYLE:
• Game Boy / classic handheld RPG nostalgia
• Premium modern pixel art
• Clean white/off-white background
• Red + orange as the main accent colors
• Dark text for readability
• Pixel borders, subtle hard shadows and retro UI details
• Lots of breathing room — NEVER clutter the page
• Avoid generic gaming websites, excessive gradients, excessive cards or long paragraphs
• Everything should feel like one coherent pixel-art universe

HEADER:
Logo: **IDLEMON REVO**
Navigation:
Home / The Game / Rarities / Economy / Gyms / Marketplace
Primary CTA: **PLAY NOW**

HERO:
Large, beautiful pixel-art scene.
Use the image provided by the user as part of a prominent image carousel.

Headline:
**IDLEMON REVO**
**Your journey begins here.**

Short copy:
“Explore maps, discover Pokémon of different rarities, manage your energy and build your own journey in a world inspired by the golden age of handheld RPGs.”

Buttons:
**PLAY NOW**
**EXPLORE THE WORLD**

Create subtle pixel particles and small interactive details.

ENERGY:
Section title:
**ENERGY FUELS YOUR JOURNEY**

Explain very briefly that Energy is one of the core resources used to explore maps.

Create a fun original pixel-art Energy icon and a small retro energy meter.

RARITIES:
Title:
**NOT EVERY ENCOUNTER IS THE SAME**

Show only these rarity names, without individual descriptions:

COMMON
UNCOMMON
RARE
EPIC
MYTHIC
BLACK MYTHIC
ANCESTRAL

Visually make the rarities progressively feel more mysterious and valuable.

Mention only:
“Different rarities can appear across different maps and encounters.”

Do NOT invent stats, percentages or abilities.

MAPS:
Title:
**EVERY MAP HIDES A POSSIBILITY**

Show a beautiful horizontal progression of pixel-art environments/maps.

Use simple visual indicators for exploration and Energy cost.

Keep the information minimal.

ECONOMY:
Title:
**THE ECONOMY OF YOUR JOURNEY**

Show four beautiful custom pixel-art icons:

GOLD
CRYSTAL
EMERALDS
RUBY

Explain simply:
“These in-game tokens are connected to the IDLEMON REVO economy and its marketplace.”

Do not use investment language or financial promises.

MARKETPLACE:
Title:
**THE IDLEMON MARKETPLACE**

Show a charming pixel-art marketplace/shop with coins, crystals, eggs and items.

Short copy:
“Trade and explore the evolving economy of the IDLEMON REVO universe.”

CTA:
**EXPLORE MARKETPLACE**

GYMS:
Title:
**GYMS ARE WAITING**

Show a memorable classic RPG-style pixel-art Gym building.

Short copy:
“Prepare your team and face the challenges waiting throughout your journey.”

CTA:
**DISCOVER THE GYMS**

GAME BOY NOSTALGIA:
Create a small emotional section:

**MADE FOR THOSE WHO MISS THE CLASSICS**

“Pixel art. Exploration. Collection. Discovery.
A simple journey inspired by the games that shaped a generation.”

Show a retro handheld device containing an IDLEMON REVO scene.

FINAL CTA:
**YOUR JOURNEY STARTS NOW.**

“Explore. Discover. Collect. Evolve.”

Button:
**PLAY IDLEMON REVO**

FOOTER:
IDLEMON REVO
The Game / Maps / Rarities / Marketplace / Gyms / Community

Small phrase:
**A journey built in pixels.**

PSYCHOLOGY + UX RULES:
• Use visual hierarchy to guide the eye naturally toward “PLAY NOW”
• Use nostalgia to create emotional connection
• Use progressive disclosure — reveal information gradually
• Use whitespace to make important elements feel more valuable
• Use red for action/brand energy and orange for discovery/highlights
• Use rarity progression to create curiosity
• Keep sections short and visually rewarding
• Use micro-interactions: button press effects, subtle pixel particles, hover animations and gentle carousel transitions
• Avoid information overload
• Keep the experience fast and intuitive
• Mobile-first responsive design
• No unnecessary pages or complicated navigation

IMPORTANT:
Create original, consistent pixel-art icons for Energy, Gold, Crystal, Emerald, Ruby, Egg, Gym and Marketplace. Do NOT use emojis as final icons.

The entire website should feel like:
**“A classic Game Boy RPG brought back to life on the modern web.”**

Prioritize:
**EMOTION → CLARITY → NOSTALGIA → CURIOSITY → PLAY**
